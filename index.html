Calculator Assumptions

  • Server and hardware costs are amortized over their lifespan
  • Power costs assume 24/7 operation (8760 hours per year)
  • Maintenance costs are calculated as a percentage of total hardware cost
  • Labor costs include only direct IT personnel
  • Network equipment is amortized over 5 years
  • Tier I: Basic capacity with minimal redundancy (99.671% uptime)
  • Tier II: Redundant components with single distribution path (99.741% uptime)
  • Tier III: Concurrent maintainability with N+1 redundancy (99.982% uptime)
  • Tier IV: Fault tolerance with 2N/2N+1 systems (99.995% uptime)

This calculator provides an estimate only. Actual costs may vary based on specific requirements, location, and implementation details.
